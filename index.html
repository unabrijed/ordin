A little clarity

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Who pays for a renewal?+

Your wallet supplies the payment tokens. The account submitting the collection, typically the merchant or its automation, pays the network fee.

What if my balance is too low?+

The collection fails without a partial payment. It can be retried while the billing period remains eligible. Missed periods don’t accumulate into debt.

Can the merchant change my terms?+

No. A different amount, recipient, or schedule requires a new owner authorization. Each policy allows only one successful collection per billing period.

Is Ordin ready to use?+

Ordin is live on Whitechain testnet for testing with test tokens. It is not audited or available on mainnet. Follow the testnet guide ↗
